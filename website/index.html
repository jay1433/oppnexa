FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you submit bids on our behalf?

No. We find and deliver the open opportunities and the contacts to pursue them. The bidding itself — the paperwork and submission — stays with your team. That's what keeps us fast and affordable.

Which countries and sectors do you cover?

Both public and private opportunities — government tenders (US federal and state, India central and state) and private/corporate RFPs — across domains including software projects, construction, IT, healthcare, facilities, services, and education, in the US and India.

How often do I get the report?

Every week. A clean, scannable report of currently-open bids lands in your inbox, matched to your company.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It's a simple monthly membership with no long-term contract.
